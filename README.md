# 🌱 GROW BY SAVINGS
## Personal Finance Tracker App

**Track Your Money, Build Your Future**

A simple, lightweight web app to track your daily transactions and manage your savings goals.

## ✨ Features

✅ **Track Transactions** - Add income and expenses with descriptions and categories  
✅ **Savings Target** - Set and track your savings goals with a visual progress bar  
✅ **Smart Summary** - View total income, expenses, and balance at a glance  
✅ **Category Organization** - Categorize expenses (Food, Travel, Entertainment, Utilities, Other)  
✅ **Data Persistence** - All data is saved locally in your browser (no account needed)  
✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops  
✅ **Zero Setup** - No installation or internet connection required  
✅ **Secure & Private** - Your financial data never leaves your device  

## 📱 How to Use

### Step 1: Open the App
- Simply open `index.html` in your web browser
- Works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and mobile browsers

### Step 2: Set Your Savings Goal
- Enter your target savings amount
- Click "Set Target" button
- Watch the progress bar fill up as you save!

### Step 3: Add Transactions
- Enter transaction description (e.g., "Coffee", "Salary")
- Enter the amount
- Choose if it's income or expense
- Select a category with emoji
- Click "Add Transaction"

### Step 4: Track Your Progress
- View your total income, expenses, and balance
- See the progress bar fill up as you approach your savings goal
- Delete individual transactions if needed
- Review all your recent transactions

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

🔐 **Fully Local** - No internet connection required after loading  
🔐 **No Data Sharing** - Your financial data never leaves your device
